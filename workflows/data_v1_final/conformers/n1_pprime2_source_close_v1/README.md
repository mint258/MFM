## Conservative outcomes

A complete package needs the real claim, dispatch intent and independently
persisted worker-start chain. UID, job, node, PID/start ticks, boot identity,
source/core/catalog/prepared/baseline authority and original seed are checked.
The original `planning.packet_readback` is called unchanged for gzip EOF/CRC,
original atom order, preserved baseline, finite candidate float64 coordinate
SHA, supplement identity and source-record checks. A present finished record
must agree with the actual packet, start and claim references. Missing finished
alone is allowed: a parent may time out after fsync of a valid packet.

Missing final producer receipt is allowed. A valid receipt's consumption claims
are evidence, while its unstarted list does not erase physical evidence.
Malformed/unattributable receipt is conservatively a conflict for the batch.
A packet with no valid claim/start, conflicting source or SHA, partial packet,
or worker-failure conflict is retained with physical locator, fresh observed
reference/error and UNKNOWN/CONFLICT. It never becomes proven-unstarted.
A dispatch-only claim remains consumed UNKNOWN. A valid failed/pending packet
can be a genuine observed result without a second geometry. No unknown result
is assigned a copied scientific status or geometry ID.

Within a registered terminal batch, only absence of every task-level durable
evidence type can yield `PROVEN_UNSTARTED_UNCONSUMED`; even that state has
`automatic_retry_authorized=false`. Unparseable claim owners or owners absent
from actual terminal accounting hold closure, while preserving observations.
The closer's C0 means the batch was fully inspected with terminal owners; it
does not mean every task succeeded or that the producer was C0.
